# SHEEPTATOR — A Sheep Management Tragedy

(Formerly "FLEECED". The code namespace and folder are still called `Fleeced`; the save file is still `fleeced_save.json` so old saves keep loading.)

A playable vertical slice of the design. Everything (pixel art, font, UI, sound) is generated by code at runtime. There are no imported assets.

## Run it
1. Open the project in Unity 6000.0.32f1 and let the scripts compile.
2. Open `Assets/Scenes/SampleScene` (any scene works) and press **Play**.
   The game boots itself via `[RuntimeInitializeOnLoadMethod]` in `Core/Game.cs`.

**Controls:** WASD / arrows or right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom, left-click to select, right-click/Esc to cancel.
Space/Enter also advance the Supreme Leader's dialogue. Space pauses, 1-3 sets speed. Panels: B Build, F Flock, O Factory, N Genetics, R Research, T Market, M Map, H Help. L toggles the building name plates. On an event card, 1-3 picks a choice.
G = open/close the main door of the selected room (or the first pen). C = conveyor tool (drag to lay, R rotates). F5 save, F9 load.

## The core loop: rooms, doors, conveyors, machines (a sheep Factorio)
- **Sheep never work. They are managed.** Every sheep is livestock: it lives in a Breeding Pen, eats in a Feeding Lot and rides conveyors through machines. It gets shorn for **wool**, milked for **milk**, bred for **lambs**, or turned into **mutton** (or sold alive at a Market). Every building runs by itself: farms grow food, labs research and markets sell, with no sheep standing in front of them. (Version 7 removed the old jobs; old saves turn every worker back into livestock.)
- **Sheep stay contained.** Every exit leads onto a conveyor and then into another building or a room. A belt that ends on bare ground, a building that takes no sheep, or a machine with no open way out makes the sheep **wait right there** on the line, and the Supreme Leader shouts about it.
- **Every building says what it does:** a name plate with in > out icons floats over it (L toggles), and the build menu, the inspector and the help all show the same "Sheep in: WOOL off, sheep out" line.
- Sheep live in **rooms**: the **Breeding Pen** (8x5) and the **Feeding Lot** (6x4). Every room has a red **DOOR** in the middle of each side (bottom, right, top, left).
- A door works when a **conveyor** starts right outside it and leads away. **OPEN** the door (button next to it, the room's panel, or **G**) and the sheep walk out one by one and ride the belt to wherever it leads: another room, a machine, or the Slaughterhouse. A belt that points INTO a room drops sheep inside (and waits if the room is full). **CLOSE** the door and they stay. The button under each door says where it leads ("TO SHEARING MACHINE").
- **Breeding Pen:** home. Sheep nibble the grass (it helps a little) and fed, happy rams and ewes make **lambs** while the pen has room. Holds 16 / 24 / 34 / 46 / 60 (5 levels) and **breeds faster every level** (x1 / 1.3 / 1.65 / 2.1 / 2.7). Matchmaking research is another x1.5; Bunk Beds / Sheep Apartments add 4 / 8 room to every pen. **The flock can never be bigger than your pens** (there are no Sheep Houses any more).
- **Feeding Lot:** sheep that ride in walk to the troughs and eat from the food store until they are **FULL**. Only fed sheep leave through its doors. Holds 8 / 14 / 22 / 32 / 48 and eats faster each level. Farms (with Farmers) fill the food store.
- **Shearing Machine / Milking Machine / Industrial Wool Plant** sit ON a conveyor: a sheep rides in and comes out the other side shaved or milked (rams get "REJECTED"). No workers.
- **Slaughterhouse:** the door nobody comes back from. Sheep ride in through its IN doors (shut them to stop it), **Mutton** comes out on a belt (or goes to storage) and sells well. The flock's mood dips while it runs. It is how you thin an overcrowded flock.
- **Market doors:** a Market (and every goods building: Creamery, Workshop, Textile Factory, Atelier, Storage) has an IN door on each side a belt runs into. Whatever rides into a Market is sold: goods at +10%, and **live sheep** for about half their price (Charismatic sheep +50%). Shut a door and everything waits on the belt.
- **The balance:** a slaughtered or sold sheep is worth one payout; a kept one keeps making wool (about 16 coins a day) and, for ewes, milk (about 19), plus lambs. Slaughter only what you cannot feed or house. The **FACTORY** panel (O) shows yesterday's numbers: lambs born vs. slaughtered / sold / dead, food grown vs. eaten, what was made, every room and what each machine did, with a one-line verdict.
- **Machine doors:** every side of a machine that a belt touches has a door (a roll-up shutter with a lamp, plus a small **IN** / **OUT** tag next to it: green = open, red = shut; click the tag or use the machine's panel). A shut **IN** door makes sheep wait on the belt. Sheep leave through **open OUT doors**; with two open OUT doors the line **splits** (round robin, and Smart Door filters work on OUT doors, after the machine has done its job). A shut OUT door is a goods hatch. If no OUT door is open (or no open one lets that sheep through its filter), the sheep **waits inside** and the line stops until you fix it. Until you click a door it follows the belts: open where a belt comes in and straight across from it, shut elsewhere. The Slaughterhouse has IN doors only; its way out is a mutton chute.
- Machines push their goods onto belts pointing AWAY from a **shut** side (never onto the sheep line). Run those belts into **Markets** (build as many as you like) and the goods sell themselves (+10%).
- **Genetics (DNA-altered sheep):** research **Sheep Genetics** (40 RP) and put a **Gene Lab** ON a conveyor. In **SAMPLE** mode every sheep that rides through gives 1 **DNA** (the Slaughterhouse also gives 1 per sheep, 3 for special ones). Spend DNA in the **GENETICS** menu (N):
  - **13 strains** (unlock with DNA, then set the Gene Lab to **SPLICE**; each splice costs 2-5 DNA, less at higher Gene Lab levels):
    - **Silkfleece**: shorn for **Silk**, 14c.
    - **Creamer**: ewes milked for **Cream**, 12c.
    - **Jumbo**: double mutton, eats 50% more.
    - **Golden Fleece**: needs 20 Silk; shorn for **Golden Fleece**, 45c, grows slower.
    - **Glowfleece**: needs 20 Cream; shorn for **Glow Wool**, 28c, glows, slightly radioactive.
    - **Turbo Sheep**: machines handle it 3x faster, walks 50% faster, racing stripe.
    - **Teacup Sheep**: tiny, eats 60% less, 60% of the wool and milk, adored.
  - **Six secret hybrids** show up as "???" in the strain book. A lamb of two different strains has a 1-in-4 chance (Strong Genes: 35%) to be the hybrid, which reveals it:
    - **Cotton Candy Sheep** (Silk x Cream): shorn for **Cotton Candy**, 20c, fleece grows faster.
    - **Chocolate Sheep** (Cream x Jumbo): milked for **Chocolate Milk**, 18c.
    - **Wolfsheep** (Jumbo x Turbo): +1.5 guard power each, 50% more mutton, sometimes eats a pen-mate.
    - **Sparky** (Glow x Turbo): every Sparky alive makes 3 power; zaps the neighbours.
    - **Splitter** (Teacup x Glow): now and then splits into two sheep if the pen has room.
    - **Phoenix Sheep** (Gold x Glow): when it dies, even in the Slaughterhouse, it is usually reborn as a Phoenix lamb.
  - Spliced sheep look different (pearly, cow-spotted, big, tiny, golden, glowing, striped, pink-and-blue, brown, grey, sparking, seamed, smouldering), are worth more alive, and their lambs keep the strain half the time.
  - **CHAOS mode** (1 DNA per sheep), when the Gene Lab spins the wheel: 45% any strain (even an undiscovered hybrid, which reveals it), or a mutation (two heads, three eyes, rainbow, golden...), BABY AGAIN (the sheep turns back into a lamb), a FLUFF BOMB (instant wool), a random trait, a burp, or 5% KA-BOOM (the sheep becomes wool and mutton; Safe Splicing halves it).
  - **The Gene Lab** shows its mode in a tank of bubbling goo, coloured by the strain it splices (rainbow in CHAOS), flashing while it works. Level 2 / 3 give 2 / 3 DNA per sample and splice 1 / 2 DNA cheaper.
  - **Breeding upgrades:** Love Songs (pens breed +30%), Quick Lambs (grown up in 2 days), Twin Genes (1 birth in 5 is twins), Strong Genes (lambs keep strains 90%), Safe Splicing (fewer side effects), Big Fleece (+25% wool growth, needs Silk), Rich Milk (+25% milk, needs Cream).
  - Splicing has side effects now and then: an extra head, a third eye, spikes, a new trait, or a faint.
  - **Rare goods upgrade things:** every building's **Level 4 needs 15 Silk + 15 Cream** and **Level 5 needs 15 Golden Fleece + 15 Glow Wool** (on top of coins and research).
  - New door filters **PLAIN** and **SPLICED** route sheep to or around the Gene Lab.
- **Growth and upgrades:** lambs keep coming, so you keep upgrading: bigger, faster-breeding pens and feeding lots (5 levels each), more farms, faster machines that get more wool / milk / mutton out of every sheep (Level 2 costs only coins; the inspector shows what the next level gives), more Markets and faster belts.
- **Belt tiers:** Rubber (5c), **Steel** (12c, research Steel Belts: 80% faster, sheep ride closer together) and **Mega** (30c, research Mega Belts: wider, fastest, sheep packed tight). Lay a better belt over an old one to upgrade it, or press **UPGRADE ALL BELTS** in the Build menu. Greased Belts research makes every belt 30% faster.
- **Smart Doors** research gives every door a **filter**: ALL, HUNGRY, FED, READY, FLUFFY, MILKY, RAMS or EWES. Example: pen door to the lot = HUNGRY, lot door to the shears = FLUFFY, lot door to the Slaughterhouse = RAMS. Leave the doors open and the factory runs itself.
- Buildings can be dropped on top of belts. Belts and buildings can't go inside a room.

## The Supreme Leader (tutorial)
A new game opens with **the Supreme Leader**: a big ram with curly horns, a fleece, goat eyes, an officer's cap, a sash and too many medals. He introduces himself as a sheep ("Welcome to my SHEEPTATORSHIP"), stands on his palace balcony, and addresses the ten hungry sheep in his Breeding Pen. You are his **Hoof**: he points, you click.

- He talks through a portrait dialogue box (typed text, a gruff blip voice, 3 expressions). He salutes from the balcony.
- The tutorial is 16 short **decrees**: inspect a sheep, repair the conveyor from the pen to the Feeding Lot (glowing tiles), OPEN the pen door, OPEN the Feeding Lot's door to the Shearing Machine once they are fed, watch the shearing, CLOSE the pen door and wait for everyone to come home, build a Farm and give it a worker, build a Market on a blueprint, lay a product belt from the machine into the Market, watch the wool sell itself, build a Milking Machine on the line, shut and reopen its IN door (machine doors), build a Slaughterhouse and open the lot's bottom door once (he closes it again himself), wait for a lamb, then a Research Lab. A bouncing gold arrow and a pulsing frame point at exactly what to click; glowing blueprints and belt plans show where things go (the conveyor tool auto-corrects the direction on planned tiles).
- Menus and buildings unlock as he introduces them, so the start is slow and readable. SKIP TUTORIAL on the first page unlocks everything.
- After the tutorial, the top box shows his **DECREES** (the goal list, with rewards), which push you to grow the factory: feed sheep, sell mutton, upgrade the Feeding Lot, a second pen, Smart Doors, Steel belts, cheese, yarn... He also **shouts** when something goes wrong: hungry pens with no open door to food, a full Feeding Lot, an empty food store, an overcrowded pen, sheep traffic jams on the belts, a machine with no belt into it, idle buildings, full storage, idle scientists, wolves, deaths and accidents.
- Version 9 adds 8 strains (6 secret hybrids), CHAOS mode, Chocolate Milk and Cotton Candy, and two hybrid decrees after "Own 3 Golden Fleece sheep" (older saves are shifted).
- Version 8 saves add DNA, strains, genetics upgrades and Gene Lab modes; five genetics decrees were inserted after "Split a sheep line" (older saves are shifted).
- Version 7 saves: sheep are livestock only (old workers return to their pen), Sheep Houses are retired (the pens are the population cap), machine Level 2 costs only coins.
- Saves from version 5 keep working: machine doors start in "follow the belts" mode, and a mid-tutorial save picks up the new machine-door lesson. Old saves that finished the tutorial get a one-time announcement.
- Saves from before version 5 skip the tutorial. On the title screen they appear as "CONTINUE OLD SAVE" under the BEGIN button. In version-4 saves a pen's old gate becomes its bottom door.

## Feeding, wool and milk
- Sheep get hungry (about 4 food a day each). In a room they only eat in a **Feeding Lot** (or by nibbling pen grass). Lambs drink milk and don't need food. Hungry sheep don't breed and grow wool slowly; starving sheep die. A Level 1 Farm grows about 48 food a day (enough for 12 sheep) by itself.
- **Wool** grows over time; **milk** fills up in ewes (a pink udder means full). Both are harvested only by machines on the conveyor. The **Creamery** (research Cheese Making) turns 3 milk into 1 cheese.

## Save / load
The game autosaves every in-game day and on quit, to `Application.persistentDataPath/fleeced_save.json`
(on Windows: `%USERPROFILE%/AppData/LocalLow/DefaultCompany/Sheepgame/`). The title screen shows CONTINUE / NEW GAME.
The Map panel has SAVE, LOAD and NEW GAME buttons. Everything is saved: sheep, buildings, belts, items on belts, terrain, research, events and pending story chains.

## Factory layer
- **Conveyor belts** (available from the start, three tiers, see above). Buildings push their products onto any adjacent belt pointing away from them. A belt that runs into a building feeds it: the building gets a local input buffer and works **+50% faster**. Belts into the Market sell everything at +10%; belts into Storage put goods in stock. Goods stuck at a dead end are carried to storage after a few seconds; goods at a SHUT door wait.
- **Sheep ride belts** in single file (closer together on better belts), queue at busy machines, shut doors and full rooms, wait at dead ends, and are dropped into a room when a belt points into it.
- The old Fluff Funnel is retired (hidden; it still works in old saves).
- **Levels 4 and 5** for 13 building types (research Hyper-Industrialization and The Wool Singularity). These are neon skyscrapers, glowing reactors and giant sheep-skull facades, much more productive (x4 / x6) and much more dangerous.
- **Bigger world:** a 96x72 map with 9 land expansions. New tiers: Sheep Metropolis and The Wool Singularity.

## What's in
- **Individual sheep:** name, gender, level/XP (earned in the machines: +5% wool and milk per level), mood (with a factor breakdown), hunger, wool growth, 23 traits that change wool, milk, mutton, live price, accidents, food and mood (Overachiever, Charismatic, Glutton, Strong...), and 6 rare variants (Golden, Black, Rainbow, Three-Eyed, Spiky, Two-Headed). The inspector shows where a sheep lives and what it is worth alive or as mutton.
- **No jobs:** sheep are never workers. Every building runs by itself at a rate set by its level.
- **22 buildings** with 3 visual levels each (wooden shack, then workshop, then rusty industrial with smokestacks, gears and blood stains). They build themselves.
- **Wool chain:** Wool, then Clean Wool, Yarn, Fabric, Clothing and Luxury Goods. Prices fluctuate and drop when you dump stock.
- **Food:** Farms grow it by themselves; pen grass helps a little and visibly wears down. Seasons matter, and winter is lean.
- **Breeding** with trait inheritance, Selective Breeding and rare-variant odds. **Cloning Vat.**
- **Danger:** machine accidents to the sheep going through (shaved, injured, launched, flattened, burned, knocked out, or dead), "Remove Safety Guards" (+35% output), pollution that kills grass, deaths with a NOOO banner, ghosts, tombstones and a memorial.
- **20+ decision-card events** with follow-up chains (vegetarian wolf Gerald, the fake luxury wool scandal, the Great Shears cult, illegal shears, and more), plus wolf raids and Guard Posts.
- **38 research projects** in 7 categories, ending in the Moon Grass Hypothesis, the Sheep Space Program, a rocket launch, and an ending card.
- Settlement tiers (Sad Little Field up to Interplanetary Sheep Civilization), a guided goal track with rewards, land expansion, and day/night with seasons.
